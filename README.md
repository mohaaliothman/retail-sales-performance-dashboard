#  Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand overall business performance and identify key patterns across products, customer segments, regions, sales channels, and time.

The data was cleaned and validated before being analyzed in Power BI. An interactive dashboard was then created to track key performance indicators (KPIs) and explore the factors driving revenue and profit.

The main goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into clear and actionable business insights.

---

## Business Questions

This analysis aims to answer the following questions:

- How much revenue and profit did the business generate?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which products and categories generate the most revenue?
- Which regions generate the most revenue?
- Which customer segments contribute the most to sales?
- How do online and in-store sales compare?
- What factors are driving differences in sales performance?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains retail transaction data covering the period from **January 2024 to December 2025**.

It includes information about:

- Orders and customers
- Products and product categories
- Quantity and unit price
- Sales, cost, and profit
- Customer segments
- Regions and cities
- Sales channels
- Payment methods
- Order dates

Both the raw and cleaned versions of the dataset are included in the repository to demonstrate the data preparation process.

---

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the dataset was reviewed and cleaned to improve data quality and ensure accurate analysis.

The main preparation steps included:

- Removed empty rows.
- Checked for duplicate records and duplicate Order IDs.
- Checked for missing values.
- Reviewed and corrected column data types.
- Validated sales and profit calculations.
- Reviewed categorical values for consistency.
- Identified and retained `Unknown` values where information was unavailable.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for analysis in Power BI.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following DAX measures were created in Power BI:

### Total Revenue

`Total_Revenue = SUM(retail_sales_cleaned[Sales])`

Measures the total sales revenue generated by the business.

### Total Profit

`Total_Profit = SUM(retail_sales_cleaned[Profit])`

Measures the total profit generated from sales.

### Total Orders

`Total_Order = DISTINCTCOUNT(retail_sales_cleaned[Order_ID])`

Counts the number of unique orders.

### Profit Margin

`Profit_Margin = DIVIDE([Total_Profit], [Total_Revenue], 0)`

Measures how much of the generated revenue is retained as profit.

### Average Order Value

`Average_Order_Value = DIVIDE([Total_Revenue], [Total_Order], 0)`

Measures the average revenue generated per order.

### Total Quantity

`Total_Quantity = SUM(retail_sales_cleaned[Quantity])`

Measures the total number of product units sold.

### Total Customers

`Total_Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(retail_sales_cleaned[Customer_ID])`

Measures the number of unique customers.

### Average Unit Price

`Average_Unit_Price = AVERAGE(retail_sales_cleaned[Unit_Price])`

Measures the average selling price per unit.

## Key Insights

### 1. Product Performance

**Office Chair** generated the highest product revenue at approximately **JOD 255.6K**.

Although Smart Watch sold more units, Office Chair had a higher average unit price, which helped it generate greater overall revenue.

### 2. Sales Channel Performance

**Online sales generated approximately 57.8% of total revenue (JOD 1.32M).**

The difference was mainly driven by a higher number of online orders, while the Average Order Value was very similar between Online and Store sales.

### 3. Monthly Revenue Trend

Monthly revenue remained relatively stable with some fluctuations throughout the analyzed period.

**February 2025** recorded the lowest monthly revenue, while **December 2025** recorded the highest.
